# Quality checklist — cadence-review

- [ ] The review opens with decisions needed this week (or states "none").
- [ ] Items closed since the last review are stated once and dropped from the running list.
- [ ] Every slip is reported with its count to date.
- [ ] Any step at or above the repeat threshold is escalated to a defect report, not re-chased.
- [ ] Blocked items name the decider, separately from overdue items.
- [ ] Every open thread has an owner and a date, or is flagged as missing one.
- [ ] "Worked on" / "in progress" appears nowhere as a state.
- [ ] There are five asks or fewer.
- [ ] Every ask names one person and one date.
- [ ] Slips found during the review were written back with `ops_log_slip`.

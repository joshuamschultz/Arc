# Quality checklist — owner-sweep

- [ ] Blocked and overdue are reported in separate sections.
- [ ] No item appears in both sections.
- [ ] Every blocked item names the person who must decide.
- [ ] Every blocked item states how long it has been blocked.
- [ ] Every commitment with no owner is reported `UNOWNED`, never guessed.
- [ ] Every commitment with no due date is reported `NO DATE`.
- [ ] Every item carries one specific ask naming one person and one date.
- [ ] Items are ordered by what stalls the most downstream work, with the rationale stated.
- [ ] The sweep leads with Blocked.
- [ ] On-track items are one line each and do not dominate the page.

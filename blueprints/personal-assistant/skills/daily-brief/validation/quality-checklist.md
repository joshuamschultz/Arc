# Quality checklist — daily-brief

- [ ] Anything due or breaking today leads the brief.
- [ ] Commitments past their date name who is owed and how late.
- [ ] The brief contains five items or fewer.
- [ ] A quiet day is stated in one line, with no padding.
- [ ] Facts and inferences are distinguished ("you told me" vs "it looks like").
- [ ] Every name, date, and amount traces to a card or memory.
- [ ] Sections with no content are omitted entirely, not shown empty.
- [ ] There is no preamble and no closing offer of help.
- [ ] The brief reads in under thirty seconds.

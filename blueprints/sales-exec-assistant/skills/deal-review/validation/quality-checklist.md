# Quality checklist — deal-review

- [ ] A one-word verdict (healthy / at-risk / slipping / stalled) is given.
- [ ] Presence or absence of a scheduled next step is stated.
- [ ] Time-in-stage is assessed against the account's norm.
- [ ] Number of threads and economic-buyer engagement are stated.
- [ ] The top risk is one specific sentence, grounded in a card or insight.
- [ ] Exactly one de-risking next action is prescribed.
- [ ] The verdict does not contradict the evidence (e.g. "healthy" with no next step).

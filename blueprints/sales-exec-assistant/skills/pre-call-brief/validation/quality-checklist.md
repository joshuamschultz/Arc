# Quality checklist — pre-call-brief

- [ ] Every person on the call is named with role and deal-role.
- [ ] The deal's stage, value, and next step (with owner) are stated.
- [ ] The last meeting's outcome is summarized.
- [ ] All open commitments touching the account are listed with who/what/when.
- [ ] Exactly one primary outcome to drive is named.
- [ ] Unknown facts are marked `unknown`, never guessed.
- [ ] The brief fits on one page.
- [ ] Single-threaded risk is flagged when only one contact exists.

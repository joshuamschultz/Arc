# Assumption audit — 2026-07-01

## At risk

**`[[move-upmarket]]` — at risk, recommend REVISE.**
Resting on `[[enterprise-close-without-soc2]]`, now `challenged`. Three of four enterprise
losses in `[[signals]]` (2026-04 through 2026-06) named the missing SOC 2 Type II
explicitly. The thesis is not wrong, but its sequencing is: the bet cannot be placed until
the audit closes. Revise the thesis to state SOC 2 as a precondition rather than a parallel
workstream.

**`[[land-and-expand-mid-market]]` — at risk, recommend RETEST.**
Resting on `[[expansion-follows-seat-growth]]`, now `untested`. This assumption was written
2025-11 with no falsification test and has never been checked. Net revenue retention has
been reported monthly the entire time, so the test is cheap: if NRR has not moved with seat
growth over two quarters, the assumption is false. Run it this week.

- `[[midmarket-acv-capped]]` — test recorded ("a third consecutive soft quarter") but not

## Holding

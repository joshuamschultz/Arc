# Quality checklist — competitive-read

- [ ] The date range covered is stated.
- [ ] Signals are split into moves (an actor did something) and conditions (the market changed).
- [ ] Every move names the actor, the action, and the date.
- [ ] Every claim carries a source; unsourced claims are labelled `unsourced`.
- [ ] Announced and shipped are distinguished, never conflated.
- [ ] At least one item is explicitly dismissed as noise, with the reason.
- [ ] Each change maps to the thesis slugs it presses on, or states "no bet touched".
- [ ] "No response" is recommended where that is the honest call.
- [ ] The read ends with the observable that would change our mind.
- [ ] Genuinely new signals were written back with `strategy_log_signal`.
